    $$d_o = 100 \text{ cm}$$
    Therefore, the image is also $100 \text{ cm}$ from the mirror.
4.  **Determine the required distance:** The question asks for the distance between the object and the image. Since the object and the image are on opposite sides of the mirror, the total distance between them is the sum of their individual distances from the mirror:
    $$\text{Distance} = d_o + d_i$$
    $$\text{Distance} = 100 \text{ cm} + 100 \text{ cm}$$
    $$\text{Distance} = 200 \text{ cm}$$

The image is $200 \text{ cm}$ from the object.

Q: In a certain region, the electric field varies with the radius away from origin by the equation Er = –6r^2 + 4r + 3, where r is given in meters and E in N/C. The potential difference between the origin and the point (3, 4) is
A) –165 V
B) –120 V
C) 64 V
D) 185 V
Kunci Jawaban Asli: D

...............  ➤ Putaran Debat 1 ...............

[ 🤖 ] GENERATOR (gemma4:e2b):
This problem asks for the potential difference ($\Delta V$) between the origin ($r=0$) and a specific point $(3, 4)$. Potential difference is related to the electric field ($\mathbf{E}$) by the line integral:
$$\Delta V = V_{final} - V_{initial} = - \int_{initial}^{final} \mathbf{E} \cdot d\mathbf{l}$$

The electric field is given in terms of the radial distance $r$:
$$E_r = -6r^2 + 4r + 3$$
Since the field is radial, $\mathbf{E} = E_r \hat{\mathbf{r}}$.

The potential difference is independent of the path, so we can calculate it along the radial path from the origin ($r=0$) to the point $(3, 4)$.

Step 1: Determine the radial distance $r$ to the point $(3, 4)$.
The coordinates are $(x, y) = (3, 4)$. The radial distance $r$ from the origin is:
$$r = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2} = \sqrt{3^2 + 4^2} = \sqrt{9 + 16} = \sqrt{25} = 5 \text{ m.}$$

Step 2: Calculate the potential difference $\Delta V$ using the relationship between potential and electric field.
For a radial electric field, the potential difference $\Delta V$ is calculated by integrating the electric field magnitude along the path:
$$\Delta V = - \int_{0}^{r} E_r dr$$
We integrate $E_r$ from the initial radius $r_1 = 0$ to the final radius $r_2 = 5$:
$$\Delta V = - \int_{0}^{5} (-6r^2 + 4r + 3) dr$$

Step 3: Perform the integration.
$$\Delta V = - \left[ -6 \frac{r^3}{3} + 4 \frac{r^2}{2} + 3r \right]_0^5$$
$$\Delta V = - \left[ -2r^3 + 2r^2 + 3r \right]_0^5$$

Step 4: Evaluate the definite integral.
$$\Delta V = - \left[ (-2(5)^3 + 2(5)^2 + 3(5)) - (-2(0)^3 + 2(0)^2 + 3(0)) \right]$$
$$\Delta V = - \left[ (-2(125) + 2(25) + 15) - 0 \right]$$
$$\Delta V = - \left[ -250 + 50 + 15 \right]$$
$$\Delta V = - \left[ -250 + 65 \right]$$
$$\Delta V = - \left[ -185 \right]$$
$$\Delta V = 185 \text{ V}$$

Alternatively, we can recognize that $\Delta V = \int_{0}^{5} (6r^2 - 4r - 3) dr$:
$$\Delta V = \left[ 2r^3 - 2r^2 - 3r \right]_0^5$$
$$\Delta V = (2(5)^3 - 2(5)^2 - 3(5)) - 0$$
$$\Delta V = 250 - 50 - 15$$
$$\Delta V = 250 - 65 = 185 \text{ V}$$

The potential difference between the origin and the point $(3, 4)$ is $185$ V.

Comparing this result with the choices:
A) –165 V
B) –120 V
C) 64 V
D) 185 V
