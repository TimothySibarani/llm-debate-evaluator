    *   **A) Zero population growth (ZPG):** This means the population size is constant (births equal deaths). This contradicts the premise that the population continues to grow.
    *   **B) Rapid growth rate:** This describes the speed of growth, not the specific phenomenon of momentum.
    *   **C) Homeostatic plateau:** This implies the population has reached a stable equilibrium, which is the opposite of continuing growth.
    *   **D) Demographic momentum:** This term specifically refers to the tendency for a population to continue growing (or shrinking) based on the momentum created by past birth rates, even if current fertility rates are at replacement level. If a population was growing rapidly in the past, it will continue to grow for some time even if fertility drops to replacement levels.

**Conclusion:** The tendency for a population to continue to grow long after replacement fertility has been achieved is called demographic momentum.
